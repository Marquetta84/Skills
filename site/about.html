Our Story

A Local Crew That Actually Shows Up

Prominent Roofing Co. was built on a simple idea: Memphis homeowners deserve a roofer who answers the phone, shows up on time, and stands behind the work. What started as a small crew of tradesmen has grown into a full-service residential and commercial roofing company — without losing the person-to-person service that got us here.

Today we handle everything from a single leak repair to a full commercial re-roof, and we still walk every finished job with the homeowner before we call it done.
