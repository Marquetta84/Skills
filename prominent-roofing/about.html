Built on Trust, Rooted in Memphis

Prominent Roofing is a locally owned roof repair company serving Memphis, TN and the surrounding Mid-South — with a mission to make roof repair honest, fast, and stress-free.

Our Story

Started by Roofers Who Were Tired of Bad Roofers

Prominent Roofing was founded right here in Memphis after years of watching homeowners get burned by rushed inspections, inflated quotes, and unnecessary full replacements when a simple repair would do.

We built our company around a different standard: show up when we say we will, tell homeowners exactly what their roof needs (and what it doesn't), and stand behind every repair with a real warranty. That approach has carried us through thousands of roofs across Memphis, Bartlett, Germantown, Cordova, and the surrounding communities.

Today, Prominent Roofing is the crew Memphis homeowners call first — for a leak, for storm damage, or just for a second opinion they can trust.
