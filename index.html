FAQ

Answers to common questions

How long does a new roof installation take?

Most residential asphalt shingle roofs in the Memphis area are finished in one to two days. Larger or more complex roofs — tile, metal, or steep slope — can take three to five. You'll get a firm timeline with your estimate, not a guess.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Every day. We document the damage, meet your adjuster on site, and speak their language on scope and pricing. You don't have to referee that conversation — that's our job.

What's included in your 50-year warranty?

Manufacturer coverage on the materials plus our own workmanship warranty on the installation. We'll walk you through exactly what each one covers before you sign anything.

How much does a new roof cost in Memphis?

It depends on square footage, pitch, material, and how much decking has to be replaced underneath. We won't quote a number over the phone we can't stand behind — the free inspection gets you a real one, in writing.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer financing options with approval in minutes, so a failing roof doesn't have to wait on a lump sum. Ask about it when we come out.

What areas do you serve?

All of Memphis and Shelby County — Midtown, East Memphis, Cordova, Bartlett, Germantown, Collierville, Arlington, and Millington — plus Southaven, Olive Branch, and Horn Lake across the state line.
